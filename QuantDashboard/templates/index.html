Expected CSV format

Eight columns of daily simple returns as decimals (0.001 = 0.1%): 

date,portfolio_return,benchmark_return,rf,smb,hml,rmw,cma

The dashboard analyses daily returns only - every annualised figure on it assumes 252 observations a year, so monthly or weekly data will read incorrectly.

Dates must be written YYYY-MM-DD and each one may appear only once. Column names must match exactly, though capitalisation and surrounding spaces are ignored, and rows are sorted by date before compounding.

Between 63 rows (about a quarter) and 25,200 rows (about a century) of trading days. A row missing its date, portfolio, benchmark or risk-free return is dropped and counted for you; a row missing only a style factor stays in the equity curves and sits out the factor regressions.

rf is the risk-free return for your market - whatever baseline you want Sharpe, Sortino and the CAPM fit measured against. The four style factors (smb, hml, rmw, cma) feed the factor regressions further down the analysis.
