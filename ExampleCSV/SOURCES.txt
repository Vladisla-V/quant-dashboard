SOURCES FOR example_returns.csv
================================================================================

File:      example_returns.csv
Rows:      2,472 daily observations
Period:    2016-08-29 to 2026-06-30
Built:     26 August 2026
Units:     Every numeric column is a simple (not logarithmic) daily return
           expressed as a decimal fraction. 0.0034 means +0.34 percent.

This file is a hybrid. The trading calendar, the risk-free column and the four
style factors are downloaded from Kenneth French's daily five-factor library and
reproduced unaltered except for the conversions noted under each entry. The two
equity columns -- portfolio_return and benchmark_return -- are calibrated
synthetic teaching series. They are not downloads of any named index; they exist
so the dashboard can demonstrate exact recoverable metrics on a real factor
calendar.

The generator is ExampleCSV/synthesize_returns.py (random seed 20260826). It
rewrites only those two columns, field by field, and passes every other column
through as text so date, rf, smb, hml, rmw and cma cannot drift.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLUMN: date
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trading days taken from the Fama/French daily five-factor file for this sample
window. Every row has a complete factor observation; no dates were interpolated,
padded or dropped to fill gaps. The calendar was kept unchanged when the equity
columns were written as synthetic returns.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLUMN: portfolio_return  -- synthetic portfolio
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SIMULATED. A growth- and quality-tilted teaching portfolio measured against the
synthetic benchmark below. It is not a reproduction of any real index.

Construction (see synthesize_returns.py):

    portfolio_excess = alpha + 1.2 * (benchmark - rf) + L . F + epsilon

where F is the row's (smb, hml, rmw, cma) vector and epsilon is idiosyncratic
noise orthogonal to the market, the factors and rf. The same GARCH conditional-
volatility path that drives the benchmark also scales epsilon, so calm and
turbulent stretches line up across the two series.

Targets, calibrated to match the dashboard's own annualization (252 trading days,
volatility as std * sqrt(252), CAGR as compounded growth):

    CAPM beta (excess returns)     1.200
    Jensen's alpha                 +2.00% per year
    annualized volatility          20.00%

Style loadings were seeded at smb +0.25, hml -0.15, rmw +0.10, cma -0.05 and
then nudged so the simple CAPM beta lands exactly at 1.2 despite the real factor
columns having a small sample covariance with the synthetic market. The loadings
the five-factor regression recovers are therefore close to, but not identical
with, those round numbers.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLUMN: benchmark_return  -- synthetic market benchmark
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SIMULATED. The market benchmark against which the portfolio is measured. It is
not a reproduction of any real index.

Construction (see synthesize_returns.py):

    1. Draw daily shocks from a GARCH(1,1) model with alpha = 0.05, beta = 0.90
       (persistence 0.95) and unconditional variance set to (15%/sqrt(252))^2.
    2. Scale the shock path so the sample annualized volatility is exactly 15%.
    3. Add a constant drift so the compounded CAGR is exactly 10%.

Targets:

    annualized volatility          15.00%
    CAGR (compounded)              10.00%


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COLUMN: rf  -- One-month US Treasury bill, daily return
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Kenneth R. French, "Fama/French 5 Factors (2x3) [Daily]", column RF, Data
Library, Tuck School of Business at Dartmouth College,
https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html,
26 August 2026.

Download: https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/ftp/
          F-F_Research_Data_5_Factors_2x3_daily_CSV.zip

Conversion: divided by 100, because the library publishes percent and the
dashboard reads decimals. No other change.

The header of that file defines the column as follows:

    "The Tbill return is the simple daily rate that, over the number of
     trading days, compounds to 1-month TBill rate. The 1-month TBill rate
     data until 202405 are from Ibbotson Associates. Starting from 202406,
     the 1-month TBill rate is from ICE BofA US 1-Month Treasury Bill Index."

So this column is the daily return on a one-month US Treasury bill. Taking it
from the same file as the factors is deliberate: it is the exact risk-free rate
the factors were constructed against, so the excess returns the dashboard
regresses are internally consistent with its regressors.

PLEASE NOTE, because it looks wrong at first glance: rf is exactly 0.0000 on 925
of the 2,472 rows, and on 58 percent of the rows dated before 2022. This is not
a placeholder. The library publishes RF rounded to two decimal places in
percent, which is a floor of 0.01 percent per day, and through the near-zero
policy rate era of 2016 to 2021 the true daily rate fell below half of that and
so rounds to zero at source. The largest value anywhere in the column is 0.0002,
which is the same rounding ceiling seen from the other side during 2023 to 2025.
Compounded across the sample the column averages 2.43 percent a year, which is
the correct order of magnitude for the decade.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Kenneth R. French, "Fama/French 5 Factors (2x3) [Daily]", columns SMB, HML, RMW
and CMA, Data Library, Tuck School of Business at Dartmouth College,
https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html,
26 August 2026.

Download: https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/ftp/
          F-F_Research_Data_5_Factors_2x3_daily_CSV.zip

Conversion: divided by 100. No other change.

    smb  Small Minus Big, the size factor
    hml  High Minus Low, the value factor
    rmw  Robust Minus Weak, the profitability factor
    cma  Conservative Minus Aggressive, the investment factor

The retrieved file states it "was created by using the 202606 CRSP database" and
its last observation is 30 June 2026.

The underlying method is described in:

    Fama, Eugene F., and Kenneth R. French, 2015. "A five-factor asset pricing
    model." Journal of Financial Economics 116(1), 1-22.

    Fama, Eugene F., and Kenneth R. French, 1993. "Common risk factors in the
    returns on stocks and bonds." Journal of Financial Economics 33(1), 3-56.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE MARKET FACTOR IS NOT IN THIS FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The dashboard derives the fifth regressor itself, as benchmark_return minus rf,
so that a portfolio can be measured against whichever benchmark was uploaded
rather than against a fixed US one.

That derived series is not the same object as the Mkt-RF column Ken French
publishes: theirs is the excess return on the whole CRSP value-weighted US
market including dividends, while this file's is the excess return on the
synthetic benchmark above. Because the benchmark is simulated, benchmark_return
minus rf will not track French's Mkt-RF closely; it is a calibrated teaching
series, not a reproduction of the CRSP market portfolio.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THE SAMPLE STARTS AND ENDS WHERE IT DOES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
End. The Fama/French daily factors are rebuilt periodically and, on the
retrieval date (26 August 2026), ended 30 June 2026. That last observation is
the last row of this file.

Start. The sample retains approximately a decade of trading days on that same
calendar, beginning 29 August 2016. The synthetic equity columns inherit this
window unchanged.

Re-running synthesize_returns.py with the same seed reproduces the two equity
columns byte for byte; the real factor columns would need to be re-downloaded if
the sample window moves.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHECKS RUN ON THE FINISHED FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Parsing. Accepted by the dashboard's own loader with no warnings, 2,472 rows, no
missing values, no duplicate dates, dates strictly increasing.

Factors reproduce the source. Each of smb, hml, rmw, cma and rf matches the
downloaded Fama/French file to within floating-point epsilon, that is, to the
full precision at which it is published. These columns were not modified when
the equity series were synthesized.

Synthetic targets hit. Through the dashboard's own functions (26 August 2026):

    benchmark_return   compounds to 2.55x, CAGR 10.00% a year, volatility 15.00%
    portfolio_return   compounds to 3.34x, CAGR 13.10% a year, volatility 20.00%
    CAPM beta          1.200   Jensen's alpha +2.00% a year   R-squared 0.810
    correlation        0.900 between the two total-return series

Five-factor loadings recovered on the full sample:

    Mkt-RF  +1.200   SMB +0.255   HML -0.129   RMW +0.112   CMA -0.048
    alpha   +2.31% a year (style premia the CAPM attributes to alpha instead)

The equity columns do not reproduce any real index session. They are not meant to.
